# The 6-minute demo

Use this when you sit down in front of a centre owner. The demo is not the
sale — the questions are. Ask first, show second.

## Before you go

- Seed the demo data so the screens are full, never empty.
- Open the app on **your phone**, not a laptop. That is how they will see it.
- Bring one printed receipt. Paper makes it real.

## 1. Ask, do not pitch (3 minutes)

Ask these and **write the answers down**:

1. How do you record who has paid tuition today?
2. At the end of the month, how do you know who still owes you?
3. Has a payment ever gone missing, or has a student insisted they already paid?
4. How long does it take you to find out what one student has paid so far?

If they describe an exercise book and a real dispute, you have a customer.
If they do not, thank them and leave — do not talk them into it.

## 2. Show exactly four screens (2 minutes)

- **Who owes** — "This is every student behind on fees, biggest first, with
  their phone number. This is the screen you open every morning."
- **One student** — "Every franc this student has ever paid, and what is left."
- **Record a payment** — record 25,000 FCFA in front of them. Takes 15 seconds.
- **The receipt** — print it, or show the SMS text ready to send.
  "Every receipt has a code. If a student ever argues, you both check the code."

Do not show anything else. Do not mention features you have not built.

## 3. Close (1 minute)

> "I set it up for you, enter your current students with you, and train whoever
> collects the money. That is **X FCFA** once. After that it is **Y FCFA a
> month**. If it is not saving you money after the first month, stop paying."

Then stop talking. The next person to speak should be them.

## Handling the objections you will actually get

**"We already use an exercise book."**
"Keep it. Write in it exactly as you do now. This just means that when someone
disputes it, you have a receipt number instead of an argument."

**"Our students pay cash."**
"Good — this records cash. It is not a Mobile Money thing. It is a receipt thing."

**"How much again?"**
Say the number once, clearly, and stop. Do not discount before they object.

**"Send me a proposal."**
That usually means no. Ask instead: "Can I set it up with your real students on
Thursday, and you pay only if you keep it after a week?"

**"Is my data safe? Can you see my money?"**
"The money never passes through me — it goes to your own account. I only hold
the records, and each centre can only see its own."

## What to do afterwards, the same day

Write in a notebook: centre name, who you spoke to, their phone number, their
answer to question 3, and yes / no / later. Ten of those is a pipeline.
Zero of those is a hobby.
